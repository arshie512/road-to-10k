YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE

Privacy, in plain words.

Road to 10K+ can read steps, active calories, and heart-rate records from Apple Health or Android Health Connect only after you tap Connect health app and approve access in the phone app. It only asks to read these three kinds of data. It does not write health records or sync in the background.

Health readings and your manual check-ins are saved in the app or browser on your device. Road to 10K+ does not upload health data to a server, use it for advertising, or share it with other people. The public website cannot access your phone’s health store.

Your watch may need to sync its readings to Apple Health or Health Connect first. The exact data available depends on your watch, its companion app, your phone, and the permissions you grant.

You can change health permissions in your phone’s settings. To remove locally saved activity and run entries, clear the website data in your browser settings or remove the app and its stored data from your phone.

Calories and heart-rate readings come from your device or health app and may be estimates. Road to 10K+ is a personal running journal, not a medical service or a substitute for professional care.
